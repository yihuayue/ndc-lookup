Data sources & notes

Sources & coverage

The tool uses RxNorm, maintained by the National Library of Medicine, for drug names, related products and NDC associations. Duplicate NDCs are counted once.

OptionSelectionWhat is included
NDC coverageCurrent onlyCodes currently linked to the retrieved products in RxNorm.
Current + historical (default)Current codes plus earlier RxNorm NDC associations for the retrieved products.
Include combination productsChecked (default)All related products, including products or packs with additional active ingredients.
UncheckedOnly products or packs with one distinct base ingredient. Products with unconfirmed ingredients are withheld and flagged as incomplete.

The combination-product option applies to single-ingredient searches in either coverage mode. Brand names, explicit combination names and specific products retain their matched scope.

Using the same NDC source as the ATC tool keeps product mappings and historical-association definitions consistent. No study-date filter is applied. Current associations do not establish current marketing availability, and historical results may be incomplete.

RxNorm API · National Library of Medicine

Base URL: https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST

Match the drug name
/rxcui.json searches active RxNorm concepts (allsrc=0): exact name or synonym first (search=0), then normalized matching (search=1) if no match is found. No fuzzy matching is used. The returned RxCUI is RxNorm’s drug identifier.
Find related products
/rxcui/{id}/related.json retrieves generic and branded drug products and packs (SCD, SBD, GPCK, BPCK) for an ingredient or brand. A specific product input is searched directly.
Filter combination products (optional)
When unchecked, searches matching one ingredient (IN or PIN) keep only products with one distinct base ingredient, using /rxcui/{id}/related.json?tty=IN. Ingredients are counted across each whole pack; salt forms are not counted separately. Brand names, explicit combination names and specific products keep their matched scope. Missing ingredient information is flagged as incomplete, and those products are withheld. Drug summary records exclusion counts; NDC mapping includes ingredient API links for retained products. This check uses current product relationships, including when historical NDCs are requested.
Retrieve current NDCs
/rxcui/{id}/ndcs.json returns the current NDC associations for each product.
Add historical NDCs
/rxcui/{id}/allhistoricalndcs.json?history=2 adds earlier associations, including those inherited from remapped concepts. History dates identify RxNorm releases, not marketing periods. Older products disconnected from the matched concept may be missed.
